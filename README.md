# Broncos Country Music Streaming Milestone Dashboard

## Project Overview

The Broncos Country Music Streaming Milestone Dashboard was developed to support the review of student achievements associated with the Broncos Country music project. This is a real project with real data and will be used by University of Colorado.

Broncos Country began as an experiential learning initiative in Professor Leo's Business Problem Solving course at the University of Colorado Denver. Students were challenged to apply business, technology, marketing, analytics, entrepreneurship, and creative problem-solving skills to the creation and promotion of an original Denver Broncos anthem. What began as a classroom project evolved into a professionally produced music release distributed across major streaming platforms and heard by listeners around the world.

Professor Leo served as Producer and Songwriter for the project, providing overall leadership, project funding, industry coordination, and creative direction while creating opportunities for students to participate in a real-world music production and marketing initiative.

The purpose of this dashboard is to provide University of Colorado leadership with a transparent and data-driven review of the song's performance across streaming platforms and geographic markets. The dashboard was created to assist university leadership in determining whether the student creators achieved the milestone of 500,000 streams and should be recognized for this accomplishment.

This report is intended for review by assistant deans, faculty members, and administrative staff involved in evaluating the project's results and determining eligibility for recognition.

The dashboard includes estimated Billboard-equivalent unit calculations for context and educational purposes. However, award eligibility is based on total streams rather than Billboard-equivalent units.

This dashboard was developed to help document those accomplishments and provide decision-makers with a clear understanding of the project's impact.


## Stream Counts Versus Billboard Units

This dashboard presents both total stream counts and estimated Billboard-equivalent units.

These metrics are not the same.

Streaming platforms report individual streams and downloads, while Billboard uses industry conversion formulas to estimate equivalent album units. As a result, a project may accumulate hundreds of thousands of streams while generating a substantially lower number of Billboard-equivalent units.

For transparency, the dashboard calculates estimated Billboard-equivalent units using the following methodology:

### YouTube and Ad-Supported Streams

Estimated Billboard Units = Total Streams ÷ 3,750

### Premium Subscription Streaming Platforms

Estimated Billboard Units = Total Streams ÷ 1,250

Examples of premium streaming platforms include Spotify Premium, Apple Music, Amazon Music Unlimited, and similar subscription-based services.

Examples of ad-supported streams include YouTube and other advertising-supported listening environments.

These calculations are intended to provide an educational estimate of Billboard-equivalent performance and should not be interpreted as an official Billboard certification calculation.

Because the purpose of this review is to recognize student achievement, audience engagement, and project impact rather than commercial chart performance, the primary metric under evaluation is total streams rather than Billboard-equivalent units.

For the purposes of this review, the recommended metric for recognition is total streams.

## Data Quality Considerations

During the audit process, two country codes were identified that could not be mapped to a recognized country:

* OU
* ZZ

These records are included within the source data but could not be assigned to a specific geographic location during the country mapping process.

Reviewers should be aware that a determination may be required regarding whether streams associated with these records should be included in the final audited count. If these streams are excluded from the final review, the resulting stream total may fall below the 500,000 stream milestone.

The dashboard displays these records transparently so that reviewers can make an informed decision regarding their treatment during the audit process.

## Dashboard Sections

### Header and Project Summary

The header introduces the Broncos Country project and provides context regarding its connection to the University of Colorado Denver, the student creators, and the overall purpose of the dashboard.

### Filters

Interactive filters allow reviewers to analyze results by:

* Song title
* Streaming platform
* Country
* Sale month range

These controls allow reviewers to validate specific subsets of the data and perform independent analysis.

### Summary Metrics

The summary cards provide a high-level overview of project performance, including:

* Total streams
* Estimated Billboard-equivalent units
* Total earnings
* Countries reached

These metrics update dynamically based on the selected filters.

### Music Video

An embedded YouTube video allows reviewers to view the Broncos Country music video directly from the dashboard and connect the analytics with the underlying creative work.

### Global Stream Map

The interactive world map displays the geographic distribution of streams. Reviewers can hover over individual countries to view detailed metrics including streams, earnings, and estimated Billboard-equivalent units.

### Milestone Tracker

The milestone tracker visualizes progress toward major streaming goals, including the 500,000 stream milestone and the long-term goal of one million streams.

### Monthly Stream Growth

This chart displays stream activity over time and allows reviewers to identify audience growth trends and periods of increased engagement.

### Platform Leaderboard

The platform leaderboard identifies the leading streaming services contributing to the overall performance of the project.

### Country Leaderboard

The country leaderboard highlights the geographic markets generating the highest number of streams.

### Streams Versus Billboard Units

This visualization compares reported stream counts with estimated Billboard-equivalent units, helping reviewers understand the relationship between audience engagement and industry reporting metrics.

### Store Summary Table

The interactive store summary table provides a detailed breakdown by platform and supports sorting, filtering, and export functionality.

### Country Summary Table

The interactive country summary table provides a detailed geographic breakdown and supports sorting, filtering, and export functionality.
